Glacier Man fight

Glacier Man stops reading the same guard list in the same order. A weighted pick, chains and tells make him less predictable, and phase 2 adds Icicle Rain and Sled Ram.

In play: 2 health phases, new throw poses with mixed aim, aimed snowballs, icicles that form on the ceiling after a ground pound, a sled that bounces off the wall, and a freeze-and-shatter death. The stage fight and the Wily Stage 2 refight both change.

Decisions

4 decisions

The Wily Stage 2 arena has 2 FloatingCanHole enemies at the ceiling, on the far left and right.

D1 · Icicle Rain under the 2 ceiling enemies in Wily Stage 2? Chosen Keep both enemies as they are; icicles do not skip their columns.

D2 · Damage to the player from this fight? Chosen Keep the master values; only the table moves.

D3 · Icicle Rain also as a panic finisher at low health in phase 2? Chosen Yes: under 25 % of phase 2, a double Icicle Rain.

D4 · Hits to kill over the 2 phases? Chosen 1-for-1 with master: each damage value is 2 × master.

Risks

Icicles are the main risk

  • risk An unfair icicle wave. A wave with no gap, or a drop on the player at the end of a sled. The branch keeps one safe gap per wave and tests it on the rig.
  • risk Other icicles change. GapingFish and IcicleTelly also use FallingIcicle. A unit test keeps their path.
  • risk The death sequence and the defeat timer. The shatter must end before the standard explosions and the stage exit. The real-level checks cover it.
