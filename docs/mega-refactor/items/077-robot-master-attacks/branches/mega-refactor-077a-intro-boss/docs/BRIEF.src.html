Intro boss, round two

The INTRO_STAGE boss gets a second health phase, hands that do more than orbit, new attacks and a richer death. The health phases are a shared AbstractBoss feature, so each later boss can use them.

In play: the Mega Buster does more damage (pellet 2, half charge 3, full charge 4). The boss bar has 2 colors: a bit that the player takes in phase 1 shows the phase-2 color, and at 0 the bar rolls over at once. The hands switch between the X1-style orbit and a drifting hover. A punch has a wind-up and a recoil. The new attacks are a slam that lines up with the player, dives to the floor or a wall, rests there as a step and sends shock waves around the walls and the floor, and a double clap in phase 2. Some orbs lead the player. The death has more steps, and the body stays on screen, as today.

Decisions

4 decisions

The tuning values (timings, weights, speeds) are first values. The play test sets them.

D1 · Which color shows phase 2 on the boss bar?

Chosen One new shared red-orange region for phase 2 of every boss.

D2 · How does the bar fill at the intro, when the world is held?

Chosen The phase-2 color shows from the start; the phase-1 bits fill over it in 2.25 s.

Replaced by the owner decision of 2026-11-15: the fill runs in passes, one per phase, last phase first.

D3 · What do special weapons do to this boss? (Today the Axe one-hits it.)

Chosen Each special weapon 2, the Axe 3, the big Reactor shot 4, explosions 1 to 2.

D4 · One branch or two?

Chosen One branch: health phases and the bar first, then the damage, then the boss.

Risks

The intro boss can get too hard

  • risk Difficulty. Two phases and new attacks on the first boss of the game. The tuning rows are first values; the play test sets them, in normal and hard mode.
  • risk A rider on a moving hand. Every hand motion now moves by velocity, also when a player stands on it. A fast spring could drop the rider. Play test, and a unit test of the platform carry.
  • risk The roll-over runs inside the world step. The hook only sets values and queues spawns; the boss reads the new phase at its next decision. A unit test covers the carry-over damage.
