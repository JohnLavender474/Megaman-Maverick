A bot drives player slot 1

With --bot follow, an AI brain writes the input of slot 1 in place of a pad. It copies P1's buttons with a short delay and walks back to where P1 was, as Tails does in Sonic 2.

In play: a second Megaman follows P1, jumps and shoots after P1, and warps back to P1 when it falls behind. With no option, nothing changes.

Decisions

4 decisions, all as recommended

A bot session uses the co-op rules today: a group camera on the mean of both players, walls at the side view edges, a warp at the bottom edge, and a shared life for each death.

D1 · What does the camera follow in a bot session?
Chosen P1 only, as in Sonic 2. The bot never moves the view.

D2 · What happens when the bot falls behind at a side of the view?
Chosen It warps to P1, as Tails does. The bot session sets the side edges to WARP (needs D1 a).

D3 · What does a bot death cost in this branch?
Chosen A shared life, as in co-op today. A free bot death comes with the slot agents (178e); a TODO marks it.

D4 · What is the launch option?
Chosen --bot follow: it adds a bot in slot 1; an error with --players 2.

Risks

A stuck bot can hold P1 at an exit

  • risk Exits and gates wait for every live player. A bot stuck in view under a high wall holds P1 there. The jump assist and the side warp (D2) free it; check 4 and 5 test it.
  • risk The delay is counted in frame time. At a low frame rate the copy is coarser. The fixed tick comes with item 010.
  • risk A plain copy goes wrong from another start point. A copied jump can miss a ledge. The steer and the jump assist correct it; the values are tuned at the play test.
