Safe warp spot

The co-op bottom warp tests its landing spot for walls, platforms, spikes and enemies. It tries the side that the partner faces, then the other side, then the partner's own spot. While no spot is safe, or while the partner is in the air, it waits, up to a cap.

In playA warped player never lands inside a wall, on spikes or in an enemy. The guide circle changes color while the warp waits.

Decisions answered 2026-11-07

Facing is read every frame, so the guide always shows the live choice (the item's recommendation). Say so if you want it read only at the warp.

D1

How long is the cap after the delay?

After the 1 s delay, the warp waits for a safe spot or a standing partner. After the cap, it lands on the partner's spot anyway. A dev key in --viewEdges can change it.

Chosen3 sCovers a few jumps of the partner; a stuck wait ends soon.
2 sShorter wait; a long jump or fall can hit the cap.
5 sFewer forced landings; a long wait out of play.
D2

What does the waiting player see?

ChosenGray guide at the best tryThe line and circle stay, gray while it waits, white when the warp is ready.
No guide while it waitsLess noise; the player cannot see why nothing happens.
D3

What counts as an enemy at the spot?

ChosenDamager overlap onlyA hurtful damager fixture that overlaps the player's body at the spot.
Also a 1-tile marginSafer from a walking enemy; more spots fail, more waits.
D4

Merge target of item 127?

ChosenDirectly into masterOne small branch, safe to ship alone.
Parent branchOnly for a large item with many branches.

Risks

  • A moving block arrives just after the warp. The test runs on the warp frame only; a moving platform can still hit the player next frame. Accepted.
  • A partner who never stands. For example a long ride on a jet: the cap ends the wait.
  • An odd block filter. The test uses the same block filters as the collision handler, so it blocks what the physics blocks.
