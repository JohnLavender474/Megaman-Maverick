Children drawn with their parent's offset

A child that code puts on its parent once per frame gets no draw offset, while its parent does. The gap between them changes each frame. A draw link on the sprites component gives the child its parent's offset. A restart of the step move fixes the one teleport that the guard misses.

In play: the GutsTank fist, the FireMet flame, the gem ring, X's sabre arc and about 10 other parts stop hitching against their parents. Megaman does not jump back at a pipe mouth. Gameplay does not change.

Decisions

3 decisions, answered

The audit found 14 children placed on a parent, 1 snap that the guard misses, and 2 cases that need a different fix (riders and bodies that move once per frame).

D1 · Where does the link live? Chosen On the sprites component. The body is the physics and world state; draw state goes on the draw side (now a rule in docs/mega-refactor/CLAUDE.md). So the snap is named in physics terms: restartStep().

D2 · Shapes Chosen The camera offset only.

D3 · Riders, and bodies that move once per frame Chosen A new item at the merge.

Risks

A link that outlives the attach draws a free child wrong

  • risk A stale link. A thrown projectile or a launched fist that keeps its link moves with its old parent on screen. Each link function gives null in the detached states, and the play checks throw and launch each one.
  • risk An orbit or a lerp on top. The gems and the cluster move on their own too. The parent's offset removes only the step part of the gap; the own motion stays as it is.
  • risk Overlap with 081f. It changes MegamanXSabreSlash.kt. A one-line link there; read 081f again before the merge.
