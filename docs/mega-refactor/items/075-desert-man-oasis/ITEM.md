## Goal

In Desert Man's stage, after Megaman leaves the darkness cave, there is an "oasis" section with water and palm tree
enemies. The section is very short, so the palm trees get little use. Make the oasis section much larger.

The section can include subtle callbacks to *Sonic and the Secret Rings* (Wii) and to the desert stages of *Super
Mario Bros. 3* (NES). The callbacks are references in the level design and mood only. Do not copy their levels or
assets.

This item changes gameplay on purpose. It is level content, not a refactor.

## Proposed scope

Not approved. The branch `SCOPE.md` decides the final scope.

  (use the `level-designer` and `level-builder` skills).
