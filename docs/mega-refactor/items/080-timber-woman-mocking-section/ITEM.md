- Status: idea
- Type: feature
- Depends on: none
- Related: `079`
- Branches: none yet

## Goal

In Timber Woman's stage, the last section has `MockingBit` and `MockingByte` enemies. The section is short and too
simple, so the enemies and their behaviors get little use and give no special gameplay. Make the section larger, with
layouts that use the behaviors of these enemies.

This item changes gameplay on purpose. It is level content, not a refactor.

## Proposed scope

Not approved. The branch `SCOPE.md` decides the final scope.

- Make the last section of `TimberWoman_16x14_v4.tmx` larger, with more `MockingBit` and `MockingByte` encounters
  (use the `level-designer` and `level-builder` skills).
- Design each new room around one behavior of the enemies (for example the eggs from `MockingByteEgg` or the nest
  from `MockingByteNest`).
