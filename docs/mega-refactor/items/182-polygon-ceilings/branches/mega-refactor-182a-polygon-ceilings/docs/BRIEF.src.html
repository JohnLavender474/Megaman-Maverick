Polygon ceilings

A new block, CeilingPolygonBlock, pushes a body down by its convex polygon, not by its bounding rectangle. A body that rises into a 45-degree V stops on the face.

In play: only in the new test map. Megaman's head and DrillHead's drill meet the V faces; no wall slide on a face. No live level changes.

Decisions

4 decisions, all on the recommendation

The 5 item questions are answered (ITEM.md, 2026-11-23). These 4 come from the scan.

D1 · Where does the head-contact record live? Chosen A typed map on the block, from each head fixture to the step serial of its last touch; the block's postProcess ends a contact that no longer touches. (Not a property key on the body: O1-R4.)

D2 · O5-R2: one more label read on every static-dynamic pair. Chosen The owner runs one --diagnostics pair (master and branch, same room) at the play test. (Not an accepted gap as in 068a.)

D3 · Where do the 2 level rules go (clearance under a polygon; no polygon where gravity turns)? Chosen docs/ways-of-working/levels.md, at the merge on master. (A validator check waits for item 003.)

D4 · Which body counts as grounded for the x push? Chosen A body with the FEET_ON_GROUND sense. (Not every body with vy ≤ 0: a falling flyer would slide sideways.)

Risks
