Decisions answered 2026-11-09

B1

How far does the slash hit?

Your art reaches 3.2 tiles in front of X on the floor. The X6 box reaches 5.25 tiles, because the X6 X is bigger.

ChosenThe artThe box of each frame is the visible slash. What you see hits.
X6 reachX6 boxes in tiles. Hits about 2 tiles past the art.
One box per kindSimple, but early and late frames hit where no slash shows.
B2

Sabre only, as in X6?

In X6 the buster does nothing to sabre blocks and MetaWheelF. Both are only in X maps, so the rule needs no character check.

SabreBlock onlyThe buster no longer breaks the block. The 9 wheels keep the buster.
ChosenBothThe X6 rule. The wheels get much harder; maybe try at a later play test.
NeitherThe buster keeps breaking blocks (10 hits).
B3

How does X move during a slash?

X6: on the floor X stops and cannot turn, and a jump cancels after 0.12 s. In the air X keeps air control, a landing ends the slash, and there is no air dash. On a wall X holds still, and a jump gives a wall kick. Damage ends every slash.

ChosenAll as X6Without the DOWN cancel, because X has no crouch yet.
X6, wall keeps slidingThe wall slash does not stop the slide.
X6, jump cut in the airA jump release still cuts the rise during an air slash.
Changed 2026-11-09At the diff review: sabre-only is now a damage gate on SabreBlock and MetaWheelF; the sabre does one static damage; each target hard-codes its damage (no X6 scaling); the X6 prefix goes from the code names (DebrisPiece, Wreck, JunkShot, TickFrames), each with a merge TODO.

My calls (tell me if you want any changed): UP must be held on the frame of the B press; X6 times, tuned at the play test; the floor plays art frames 1 to 7 (the stand frames go); the targets' own damage timers, about 3 hits per slash; D1000Core is stunned by a slash; Megaman's claw sound until there is a sabre sound; a new test room sabre.

Risks

  • UP + B fires a shot or a charged shot. All 3 B handlers change, and a test checks each one.
  • A slash left in the world. The sabre part destroys it on every end: damage, death, room change, level reset. Tests for each.
  • Holes in the floor body. Floor body frames 3 to 6 are cut out under the slash, so the body must never show without the slash.
