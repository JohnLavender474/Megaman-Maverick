Decisions answered 2026-11-06

D1

Which input starts X's air dash?

X6 starts the air dash on a jump press in the air, and also on the dash button. Item 069 said "A is only jump and wall kick", which does not match X6.

A again in the airThe X6 rule and Megaman's default. No new button.
ChosenMegaman's input + the dash buttonA in the air, SELECT when set to air dash, and a new DASH button now (keyboard U, pad R1). Megaman ignores DASH.
D2

What shape does the air dash have?

You said "similar to Megaman's air dash". The X6 dash and Megaman's dash differ.

ChosenX6 shapeA short hover, then a fixed 0.3 s at 7.7 tiles/s (2.3 tiles), no end bump. Matches "X6 values as they are".
Megaman's shapeHold for 0.1 to 0.25 s at 10 tiles/s, with the end bump.
D3

Which wall rules?

The X6 intro level has single-wall climbs of 10 and 14 tiles.

ChosenX6 rulesA short grab, a fixed slide speed, a kick on any wall touch (also while rising), a 0.167 s kick lock, a push-off on release.
Megaman's rulesSlide only while falling, and kick only from a slide, with the X6 numbers.
D4

Sounds for the grab, the kick and the dash?

ChosenNone for nowAs for Megaman today, with a TODO.
Existing soundsPick from the game's sound files now.

Risks

  • Megaman's play can change. 232 lines of his wall slide and air dash move behind params. Baseline tests first, and a control play check with water, Rush Jet, claws and SELECT as air dash.
  • X's body stops a kick or a dash today. It sets the side speed to 0 when X does not run. Fixed in this branch, with tests.
  • The X6 windows are short. 0.133 s grab and 0.167 s kick lock. The entry into a 2-tile opening in the X6 intro level can be too hard; a play check.
