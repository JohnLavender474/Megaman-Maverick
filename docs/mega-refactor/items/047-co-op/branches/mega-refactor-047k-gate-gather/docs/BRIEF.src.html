A gate opens only when every player is at it

Shared screen phase 4. With 2 players, a gate waits until every live player stands in it, as a side room exit does. A horizontal gate then pans each player at its own height.

Changed 2026-11-17 (D4 to D7): a player who is beaming in counts, and a respawn waits during a gate; see Decisions.

In play: with 2 players, one player at a gate waits; the gate opens when the partner arrives (or dies). With 1 player, nothing changes.

Before and after

The gate decides, not the first touch

Code sketch

The gate checks its fixture each frame

Today, GateRule:

if (entity.isPlayer) {
    val gate = gateFixture.getEntity() as Gate
    if (gate.isTriggerable()) gate.trigger()
}

After (one rule for 1 and 2 players):

// GateRule (BEGIN and CONTINUE)
if (entity.isPlayer) gate.markTouched(entity.asPlayer!!)

// Gate update, before the OPENING step
if (isTriggerable() && everyLivePlayerTouched())
    trigger(axisOnly = direction.isHorizontal())
touchedThisFrame.clear()

The gate's NEXT_ROOM_REQ carries AXIS_ONLY; MegaLevelScreen passes it to transitionToRoom.

Decisions

7 decisions

A scan of 72 live gates found a map block on the gate rectangle at 71 of them, so a player waits in the gate fixture without a new wall.

Chosen D1: in Gate, as one rule for any player count. GateRule reports touching players on BEGIN and CONTINUE; the gate opens when every live player touched it.

Chosen D2: a held player counts; the gate waits until it warps back.

Chosen D3: horizontal gates pan axis-only; UP gates keep one shared point until phase 3.

Added after the review before the play test:

Chosen D4: all or nothing. A player counts from the start of its spawn-in (beaming in); a respawn waits while a gate opens and pans.

Chosen D5: a player who stands in a locked gate opens it at once when it is unlocked, also with 1 player.

Chosen D6: a horizontal gate whose rooms give a vertical pan fails fast with a clear message (no live gate does).

Chosen D7: accepted edge case: the touched set is cleared per frame, not per world step.

Risks

A gate can wait for a partner who cannot come

  • risk A partner who cannot reach the gate. The gate waits until that player arrives or dies. The slot respawn puts a dead player next to the partner.
  • risk One gate has no map block (Gauntlet1). A player can walk past it today; not changed here.
