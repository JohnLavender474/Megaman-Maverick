Decisions answered 2026-11-06

D1

When everybody is at the exit, how does the transition start?

ChosenAt onceThe part starts the transition, the same call a gate uses. Nobody can slip through an open wall.
Open the wallSlot 0 walks through and starts it as today. Slot 1 can also walk through and leave it alone if slot 0 turns back.
D2

Exit walls in rooms with a camera zone or scroll?

ChosenOnThe wall stands at the room edge and does not move, so the reason the view edge rules are off there does not apply.
Off, as the view edge rulesSlot 1 can still leave alone from coop_hscroll and coop_vscroll, until phase 5.
D3

Only one player alive (slot 1 died)?

ChosenNo wallsThe transition works as in single player.
Walls stayThe lone player touches the exit, and the transition starts. The same rule, but a stop at each door.
D4

What counts as "at the exit"?

ChosenTouching the wallAs in your words. Simple to see and to test.
Touching and pushing toward itA player who just stands at the wall does not count. Fewer starts by accident, but a player must hold the direction.

Risks

  • Gates. A gate starts its own transition when any player touches it. An exit that a gate covers gets no wall, so the gate still works; gates with 2 players are phase 4.
  • Vertical exits. A ladder, a jump or a fall into another room still lets one player leave alone (phase 3). The new route has none.
  • One spot. After the transition, both players stand on one spot, as today.
