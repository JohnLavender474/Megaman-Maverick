Decisions answered 2026-11-06

D1

Does the group camera follow the midpoint on both axes?

With 1 player, the camera follows the player on both axes. With 2, the up-down position must also come from somewhere.

ChosenMidpoint on both axesThe view shows both players up and down as well, as long as they fit.
X midpoint, Y follows slot 0The up-down view stays as for 1 player; slot 1 leaves the view more easily in a tall room.
D2

When slot 1 dies, how does the camera move to slot 0?

Today slot 1 stays dead until the next level reset (047c adds a respawn). The midpoint then jumps by up to half a screen.

ChosenEaseUse the smooth follow that already exists, for each change of the live players.
SnapSimpler, but the view jumps at once.
D3

Group walls in a turned room, or while a camera zone or scroll acts?

A scroll moves the view by itself, so a group wall could push a player. Camera sections are phase 5.

ChosenWalls off there, with a TODOThe 051 edge blocks act as today. Phase 5 decides the rule.
Walls on everywhere nowMore cases in this branch, and a rule for a pushed player is needed first.
D4

Tall rooms in phase 1?

In a room taller than the view, a player can leave the view up or down. The rule for this is an open question in 047.

Observe onlyNot chosen.
Build one warp rule nowNot chosen.
Chosen (changed 2026-11-06)A rule for each view edge, set by a dev flagEach edge: NONE, WALL, BOUNCE, WARP (1 tile beside the partner) or KILL, with delay and damage (default 0). Default: sides WALL, top and bottom NONE. Example: --viewEdges top=WALL,bottom=WARP,delay=1.5. To discuss more after the play test.

Risks

  • One frame of lag. The camera moves after the physics step, so the walls act one frame late. A fast player (air dash) can be past the edge for one frame; the block then pushes them back.
  • Vertical spread. In a tall room, a player can still leave the view and lose its floor. The tall test room shows it; D4.
  • 1-player path. It must not change. The group focus and the walls are only built with 2 players, and a control check covers it.
