Why WARP feels wrong

Two play-test reports: a player below the view falls through a culled floor and dies before the warp, and a side warp snaps the camera. The diagrams show what the code does today, frame by frame.

1. Below the view: the floor culls first group_tall, bottom=WARP, delay=1

sequenceDiagram
  participant C as Climber (slot 1)
  participant Cam as Camera (group midpoint)
  participant F as Floor block
  participant L as Low player (slot 0)
  participant E as CameraViewEdges
  C->>Cam: climbs, midpoint rises
  Cam-->>F: floor leaves the view
  Note over F: cull timer starts (1 s out of view)
  Cam-->>L: view bottom passes over L
  E->>E: L fully below the view, warp timer starts (1 s)
  Note over F,L: about 1 s later
  F--xF: culled (destroyed)
  L->>L: no floor, falls
  L--xL: falls out of the room bottom, dies
  Note over E: the warp timer never reaches 1 s

The floor leaves the view before the player does, so its 1 s cull ends first. Any delay of about 1 s or more loses this race. The KILL rule has the same race, but the result is the same death.

2. Side warp: the camera snaps group_wide, left=WARP, right=WARP

sequenceDiagram
  participant A as Player A
  participant B as Player B
  participant E as CameraViewEdges
  participant Cam as Camera (group midpoint)
  A->>A: runs right, out of the view
  E->>A: warp beside B (same frame, delay 0)
  Note over Cam: midpoint jumps by half the spread
  Cam->>Cam: follows the new midpoint at once (SNAP)
  Note over Cam: the 0.5 s ease runs only when a player joins or leaves the group, not on a warp

3. Both out at almost the same time group_wide, sides WARP

sequenceDiagram
  participant A as Player A
  participant B as Player B
  participant E as CameraViewEdges
  A->>A: out at the left edge
  E->>A: warp beside B
  B->>B: out at the right edge (A now stands beside B)
  E->>B: warp beside A, so both end where B left the view

Both players also count in the midpoint while one is out of view. So the player who stays in view drags the view, and both players steer the camera all the time.

Fix options F1, F2, F4 built; F3 is a backlog item

F1 also draws a white line and a circle to the warp spot while the player is held. The floating sprite and the bubbles are phase 2.

F1

A player who is out at a WARP edge is held, and leaves the group

While out, the player is frozen in place (no gravity, no input) and blinks, until the warp. The camera follows only the players in view, so the player in view can never leave it, and two players can never be out together. A culled floor no longer matters. All the code is in the 2 new camera parts.

F2

The group focus eases on any large jump, not only on a join or a leave

A warp moves the midpoint by up to half a screen in one frame. With F2, any jump larger than about 1 tile in one frame starts the same 0.5 s ease. This fixes the snap in diagram 2.

F3

Keep a player's surroundings alive out of view

Cull only what is out of the view and far from every player (the "virtual camera" idea). This is the real fix for a player who is out of view, and split screen (094) needs it anyway. But it changes the cull rule of about 2270 blocks and 88 entity files, so it belongs in its own item.

F4

Side WARP stays experimental

The default for the sides stays WALL. A TODO marks side WARP as not tuned, until more play tests show whether a group camera or a leader camera suits it.
