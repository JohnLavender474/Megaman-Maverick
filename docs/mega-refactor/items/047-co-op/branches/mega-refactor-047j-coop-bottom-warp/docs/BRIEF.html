Co-op bottom warp

Co-op gets bottom=WARP as its default view edge rule, so a tall room never loses both players and never drops the low player through a culled floor.

In playIn a room taller than the view, the camera keeps the higher player in view; a player who drops below the view is held and appears beside the partner. Rooms one view tall, and 1 player, do not change.

Before and after coop_shaft, one player on the ledge, one drops

Today: bottom=NONE

  participant Cam as Camera (midpoint)
  participant L as Low player
  Cam->>Cam: shows neither player
  L->>L: floor culls out of view
  L--xL: falls out of the level, dies

After: bottom=WARP

sequenceDiagram
  participant Cam as Camera (keeper)
  participant L as Low player
  Cam->>Cam: keeps the ledge player in view
  L->>L: fully below the view: held
  L->>L: after the delay, beside the partner

Added at approval changed 2026-11-06

A held player with no free partner (slot 1 died) now dies too, so the level resets. Before, slot 0 stayed held forever: a soft lock that the new default makes easy to reach.

Risks

  • Camera feel. In every tall co-op room the camera follows the higher player more than the midpoint. Intended.
  • A pit inside a tall room. A player who goes fully below the view before the pit's death sensor is held and warps in place of dying. The review checks the tall rooms of the real stages.
  • Warp into a solid (127). More frequent with this default.
