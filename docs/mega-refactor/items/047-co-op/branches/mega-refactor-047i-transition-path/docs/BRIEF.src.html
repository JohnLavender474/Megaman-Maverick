Item 047 · Branch 047i · Objectives O2 · approved 2026-11-17

Each player keeps its own height at a gathered exit

At a side room exit that every player touches, the room transition moves each player only along the exit axis. A transition that 1 player starts (1-player play, a gate, the room scan) keeps the one shared point.

Changed 2026-11-17 (D3): the transition owns the axis-only mark; LevelState gets no field.

In play: with 2 players, a player who jumps into the exit wall pans into the next room at its own height and then falls. It no longer jumps to the other player.

Before and after

Only a gathered exit gets per-player paths

Code sketch

The player sets one axis when the exit is gathered

Today, MegaPlayer.playerOnRoomTransition:

val position = event.getProperty(ConstKeys.POSITION, Vector2::class)!!
body.setCenter(position)

After:

val position = event.getProperty(ConstKeys.POSITION, Vector2::class)!!
val axisOnly = event.getProperty(RoomTransitionEventKeys.AXIS_ONLY)!!
if (axisOnly) body.setCenter(position.x, body.getCenter().y)
else body.setCenter(position)

In CameraRoomExits, when every player is at the exit (changed by D3):

roomBounds.transitionToRoom(
    camera, exit.next!!.name, exit.side, axisOnly = true
)

Decisions

4 decisions

The player must know that the exit is gathered. The rule O1-R4 asks for a typed field, not a new event key.

Chosen D1: a typed field LevelState.gatheredExitSide. Replaced by D3.

The jump into the exit wall in coop_start already shows the fault. A standing height difference needs a ledge at an exit.

Chosen D2: add a room pair coop_ledge and coop_ledge_next with a 3-tile ledge at the right exit.

After a review by 6 agents, the field turned out to describe one transition, not the level or the co-op mode.

Chosen D3: the transition owns the mark. CameraRoomTransition.axisOnly is cleared at the end and on cancel, and it goes to the players on the transition events.

Chosen D4: the other LevelState findings (a floating-points combo bug, copied state, values kept after a level) become new items on master after this branch.

Risks

A player keeps a height that the next room may block

  • risk A block at the player's height in the next room. The height is inside the exit span of both rooms, but a block next to the edge can hold the player. Slot 0 has the same risk today.
  • risk The engine gets a flag for one core caller. "Players follow the pan on its axis only" is a genre idea, so the engine may hold it. Tests check that the end and cancel clear it.
