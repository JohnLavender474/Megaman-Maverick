Charged shot moves in 45-degree steps and never turns its pixels

The charged shot, its explosion and its residual draw only at multiples of 90 degrees. The shot moves only in the 8 directions, and a diagonal shot draws a new diagonal region.

In play: a bounced charged shot leaves at 45 degrees (today about 27 degrees off a wall, or 63 degrees after a shot down). On a diagonal, the shot and its explosion show drawn diagonal art, not turned pixels.

Before and after

The angle is rounded once, and the region choice keeps every turn at 90

Code sketch

The bounce rounds its result; the sprite picks a region

Today, ChargedShot:

temp.nor().scl(trajectory.len())
trajectory.set(temp)
...
sprite.rotation = trajectory.angleDeg()

After:

trajectory.set(temp).setAngleDeg(roundToStep(temp.angleDeg(), 45f))
...
val angle = trajectory.angleDeg().roundToInt()
val diagonal = angle % 90 != 0
animKey = if (diagonal) "diagonal" else "straight"
sprite.rotation = (if (diagonal) angle - 45 else angle).toFloat()

The same rounding runs at spawn, and in the explosion on its ROTATION prop. This is the HomingMissile pattern.

Decisions

3 decisions, answered

The shot needs 2 new diagonal strips (full and half, 2 frames each). The explosion art faces the travel direction, so a diagonal explosion needs its own frames or a turn to an axis. The residual appears only at the start of a shot, which today is always on an axis.

Chosen D1 · Diagonal explosion strips (full and half, 3 frames each).

Chosen D2 · Diagonal residual strips too (full 4 frames, half 2 frames).

art.md puts art in its own branch, and the first Fable agent of a branch needs your confirmation.

Chosen D3 · In this branch, by one Fable art agent. Fable use confirmed.

Risks

The bounce feel changes on purpose

  • risk Steeper bounce. A wall bounce climbs at 45 degrees, not 27, so a bounced shot reaches other targets. The play checks cover a wall bounce, a shot down and a turned gravity.
  • risk Atlas repack. New frames go into Projectiles1 and Explosions1. A script checks that each old region keeps its name and pixels.
  • risk Wrong turn at one diagonal. A sign error shows only at 1 of the 4 diagonals. A unit test covers the 8 angles, and the play checks show all 4 diagonals.
