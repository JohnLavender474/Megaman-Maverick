Decisions

3 decisions

The 5 backgrounds are 3 frame sheets of 512 x 448 and 2 images of 256 x 224.

Chosen D1: a new atlas Backgrounds11.

Chosen D2: the script places the decor; the owner adjusts in Tiled.

Chosen D3: a random wait from services.random.

Risks

The remap must keep every block and spawn

  • risk Map repaint. A wrong gid shows a wrong tile. The script checks that every non-tile object is unchanged and that each painted cell maps to a tile.
  • risk Atlas repack. Decorations1, Specials1 and Environs1 are repacked; other levels use them. A script checks that every old region keeps its name and pixels.
  • risk 077d overlap. 077d redraws the beamer emitter tiles; after this merge it edits them in the merged tileset, not in Reactor_Man_3 only.
