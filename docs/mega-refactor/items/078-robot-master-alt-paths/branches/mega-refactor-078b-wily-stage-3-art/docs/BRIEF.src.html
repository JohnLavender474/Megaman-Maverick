Wily Stage 3 art make-over

The final stage trades its generic brick tiles for one new lab tileset in the mood of the X6 Gate stages, from art rounds 1 and 2. A lab conveyor belt type replaces the Glacier Man belt art in this stage.

In play: new tiles, a new decor layer (trace, emblem plates, vents), 3 new backgrounds, and new belts in Wily Stage 3. Block geometry, spawns, hazards and belt push do not change.

Risks

Wrong tiles in edge cells are the main risk

  • risk A tile can look solid in air, or open in a wall. The round-2 checks rerun on the final map; 1439 cells outside the 16 rooms are in no preview, so the check covers them by script.
  • risk A background can pop in a room pan. The show/hide triggers fire on spawn, as in Wily Stage 1. The play checks pan through each change.
  • risk The Platforms1 repack can shift old regions. A script checks that every old region keeps its name and pixels.
