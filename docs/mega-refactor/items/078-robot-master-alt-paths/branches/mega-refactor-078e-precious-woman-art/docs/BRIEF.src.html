Precious Woman: a jewel mine in a crystal abyss

The round-3 art goes into the game: one new original tileset in place of 5 old ones, no back-wall tiles, and a parallax abyss background lit by the lake below and daylight from above. The moving PreciousBlock gets new frames. No Kotlin change: backgrounds are map data.

In play: the whole stage looks new. Blocks, spikes, enemies and rooms play the same.

Risks

The background must line up with the map at every start room

  • risk Far bands out of place. The 7 far textures are fixed to map rows. A wrong offset shows the lake or a band seam in the wrong room. A script renders each room from the object data and compares it with the round-3 previews.
  • risk Air reads as solid, or rock as air. The round-3 checks (contrast limits, block cells, no level edges) run again on the final files.
  • risk Platforms1 repack shifts colors of other sprites (seen in 078b). The repack check compares every old region.
