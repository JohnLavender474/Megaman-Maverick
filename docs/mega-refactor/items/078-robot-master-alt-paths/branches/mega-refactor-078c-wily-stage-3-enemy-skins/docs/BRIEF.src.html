Wily Stage 3 enemy skins

The enemies of Wily Stage 3 get a lab sprite skin in the teal, cyan and violet palette of the new tiles, drawn by Claude Fable 5.2. Each map placement picks it with color=lab, as Wily Stage 1 does with color=wily.

Changed 2026-11-19: the scope adds lab skins for InfernoOven, RisingLavaRiver and its FloatingEmber sparks (molten lava in the furnace palette); Inferno Man's stage keeps the default. Also 2026-11-19: a lab skin for GravitySwitcharoo and its aura; Moon Man keeps the default.

In play: Bat, IceFox, ShieldAttacker, UziJoe, AstroAssAssaulter, BunbyTank and WanaanLauncher (and the shots they fire, per D3) look new in Wily Stage 3 only. No behavior changes.

Risks

Atlas repacks are the main risk

  • risk Enemies2 is png8. A repack shifts the colors of some old regions slightly, as in Wily Stage 1. A script checks names, sizes and alpha.
  • risk A pooled shot keeps the skin of its last spawner. Each spawn sets the skin in onSpawn, and the default stays when a spawner gives none.
  • risk A recolor can hide a tell. The art lead keeps the light and danger cues (eyes, muzzles, warning lights) readable on the new tiles.
