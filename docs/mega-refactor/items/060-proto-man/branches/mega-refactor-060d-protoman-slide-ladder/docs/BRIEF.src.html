Proto Man: slide and ladder, with Megaman's rules

Proto Man slides on DOWN+A and climbs ladders. Megaman's slide moves into players/, so both call one shared slide; Proto Man is the third caller of the shared climb. Megaman slide tests come first, on the old code.

In play: Proto Man slides and climbs on REFACTOR_TEST_060, with new art. Megaman and X play the same.

Decisions

All answered on 2026-11-14

  1. Shared rules: the slide moves to players/; Megaman tests first; Proto Man's own values with Megaman's numbers; the slide boost stays Megaman's; Megaman's ladder rules; no after-images; no stacked ladder rooms.
  2. B in a slide: buster slot, a slide shot; sabre slot, the slide ends and the ground slash starts (nothing under a low ceiling).
  3. DOWN in the air: grabs a ladder at his feet; elsewhere, the air guard.
  4. Guard: the slide starts from the crouch guard and drops the shield at once; DOWN held at the end pulls the guard out again; no guard on a ladder; B on a ladder is always the buster.
  5. Art: 6 strips by a separate art agent; a large white shield on his back on the ladder; 2 scarf frames in the slide.

Risks

The guard must learn 2 new states

  • risk Guard readers. The guard, shield, crouch box and crouch attack read DOWN; without a change, the shield comes up on a ladder and the crouch box overrides the slide body. A test per reader.
  • risk The slide move. No slide test exists today. The Megaman tests are written and green on the old code before the move.
  • risk Art wait. The slide and climb code needs the 6 strips and their muzzle points; until then the smoke test fails.
