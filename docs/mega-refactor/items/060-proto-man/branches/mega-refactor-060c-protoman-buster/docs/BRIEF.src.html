Proto Man: sabre and buster slots, a crouch guard and crouch attacks

Changed 2026-11-13 (owner, after a neutral review of the moveset): UP+B for the buster goes. Proto Man has 2 weapon slots on SELECT: the sabre (3-slash ground combo) and the Proto Buster, which shoots while he stands, runs and jumps. Play test 5 (scope change 3): the shield is raised by holding DOWN, covers his full body, and comes out with 1-2 pull-out frames; he stands still while guarding. DOWN+A will be the slide (060d). The HUD icon sits under the health bar. Play test 6 (scope change 4): on the ground the guard is a crouch: his damage box gets 19 art px high, so shots at head height pass over him. The front shield is 7x18 art px on the ground and in the air, and the back shield is a little bigger. Pull-out: 4 frames on the ground, 3 in the air. On the DOWN release the shield drops at once and a put-away animation plays, which B, A, left and right cut short. Play test 7 (scope change 5, changed 2026-11-14): the shield is strapped on his back in the plain poses, and B with DOWN held on the ground attacks from the crouch: a low half-strength slash (sabre slot) or the standing shot at crouch height (buster slot). The attack drops the guard; with DOWN still held, the pull-out runs again after it.

In play: on REFACTOR_TEST_060 only. A HUD icon shows his slot. X and Megaman play the same.

Decisions

All answered (13 and 14 Nov)

  1. Shield (changed at play tests 5 and 6): held on DOWN, ground and air; a crouch on the ground, so head-height shots pass over; a 7x18 shield; pull-out frames (4 ground, 3 air) and put-away frames; he stands still while guarding; no guard on a ladder. Uses the existing SHIELD rule, plus a WhiteBurst spark.
  2. Buster input: weapon slots on SELECT, with SELECT_PING_SOUND and a HUD icon. New item 153 extends the icon to every player and fades the HUD behind a player.
  3. Buster: almost Mega Man's: shoots anywhere, no lock, no charge; UP+B does one slash. On a ladder, B is always the buster (Proto Man's climb is 060d; X's rule goes to 081g).
  4. Damage: half charge on hits 1 and 2 and the air slash, full charge on hit 3. Shields still do not block the sabre.
  5. Crouch attacks (14 Nov): DOWN+B on the ground: one low slash (half strength, no chain) or the standing shot from the crouch; the shield goes on his back; he stays crouched to the end of the attack, then the pull-out runs again while DOWN is held.

Risks

3 risks

  • The shield fixture must sit in front of his damage box, so a shot hits the shield first, as with SniperJoe. A fast shot could still reach the body in one step.
  • The per-move damage touches every class-keyed table that names PlayerSabreSlash; X's values must not change.
  • The restored shoot frames come from the art removed on 12 Nov; they must match the current v2 style.

  • ./run-game-alpha.sh --level REFACTOR_TEST_029 --room enemy_aim: Megaman and his HUD are the same.
