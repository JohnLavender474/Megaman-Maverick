Decisions

All answered on 2026-11-15

  1. D1, DOWN+B: the crouch shot. The crouch slash code goes; its frames stay in the atlas.
  2. D2, UP+B on the ground and in a slide: the 3-slash combo; B during a slash chains it.
  3. D3, HUD icons: deleted, and with them the whole Hud atlas (it holds only these 2); 153 makes it again.
  4. scope change 1, 2026-11-16 Sabre damage on large enemies: 10, or 12 for a full slash (was 4 and 5). One floor combo kills Robbit; X's sabre gets the same values. One slash can still hit an enemy 2 or 3 times (owner: keep it).
