# D-1000 fight

The full D-1000 fight as one state diagram: the body (`D1000`) and the eye (`D1000Core`) run at the same time, so
they are two parallel regions of the fight. Each body action shows its steps (the step enums in `D1000.kt` and the
`D1000Move` classes in `d1000/`).

The values in the labels are the Normal values on 2026-11-12 (round 15 of branch `081f`). The source of truth is
the code: `d1000/D1000Tuning.kt` for the timings and counts, `d1000/D1000Director.kt` for the weights and combos.
The package design is in `d1000/README.md`.

## Rules that the diagram does not show

- **Director.** With X within 2.3 tiles of the body, it slams (CROUCH) first, if it did not slam last. Else it
  rolls by weight, and cues add weight: X far (Ram, Walk, Bomb), X on a wall (Ram, Bracket), X above the body
  (Ceiling, Spread). It never repeats an action 3 times, never repeats a big move (Fence, Ceiling), and never
  plays 2 orb actions in a row. Bomb and Sweep come only in Overdrive. Dive comes only with X near the eye.
- **Trap guard.** Before a plan, the guard forecasts the action's dangers (body boxes, pillars, debris, eye
  shots) and refuses an action that leaves X no escape. During a volley it checks each orb again at its release.
  Orb actions (Bomb, Bracket, Sweep) are refused while X's pocket is under 7 tiles. The eye holds its pot shots
  while the body plans a volley or a Ram.
- **Orb roles.** Each volley runs a role script (`D1000Orbs`): a hunt mark aims at X with the lead, a cut mark goes
  3 tiles ahead of X's run, a feint goes 4 tiles or more from X and the earlier marks, and a Fence sweep mark takes
  the next grid lane. Bracket picks one of 3 scripts, Sweep one of 2. The Fence sweeps every grid lane from X's lane
  outward, with a hunt (snapped to the nearest grid lane) between 2 sweeps. In Overdrive, when a follow-up is
  queued, the last mark herds X: it cuts on the side away from the body, and the follow-up is told at that lock.
- **Wall volley.** The body tracks the share of the last 6 s that X spent off the floor (wall slide, jumps,
  air dash). Above 40 %, the Wall volley gets weight 25; else it is 0, so vertical volleys stay the default. The
  body rises to the top, the eye takes a spot 2.5 tiles from the wall far from X and beams sideways, and each orb
  hits that wall and makes a pillar from wall to wall at the marked height: H C H or C H C (hunt: X's lead height;
  cut: 2.5 tiles above or below). Every height is above X's standing band and below the body, so X on the floor is
  always safe; the trap guard does not count sideways pillars.
- **Combos.** Every few cycles (`comboGap`) the plan carries a follow-up that starts at once when the first
  action ends, if the guard still allows it: Ram to Crouch, Summon to Ram, Crouch to Bracket, Fence and Sweep to
  Ram. The lamp tells the follow-up 0.4 s before it starts.
- **Damage.** The eye is the weak point: buster 1, half charge 2, full charge 4, sabre 2 and a stun. The
  shielded body takes buster 1, charge 2, sabre 1. In the stun's down window the body takes full damage. The
  eye's own health is the body's health.
- **Overdrive.** The first THINK at 15 HP or less runs ENRAGE once, then every value comes from the Overdrive row
  of `D1000Tuning` (faster walk and ram, shorter think and wind-up, more orbs and debris).
